Current Sagas / Storylines

These are the active, ongoing narrative arcs currently shaping the Rise Of Seron universe. Each saga contains multiple interconnected stories that build the larger canon.

Saga 1: The Zavara Heist Saga

The first major saga following the discovery of Prism Order activity and the traitor within the Guardians.

The Zavara Heist

Seron, Tom, and Cody uncover Arsenio’s hidden operations and the Prism Order’s return.

Dunes Of Time

Casey Vigor becomes stranded in the Kacia Desert and discovers a buried Vexx research station.

The Prism Order

Seron, Tom, and Cody explore the Prism Order’s lab and uncover the rogue Guardian conspiracy.

Reach For The Stars

Tom Letanio discovers the truth — Vlasta Jeronim is the traitor working with the Prism Order.

Under Your Knife

The finale — Tom hunts down Vlasta in the Aether Mines and ends the Prism Order for good.

Saga 2: The Blood Moon Saga

The second saga, beginning immediately after the Zavara Heist, exploring the mysteries of OverDarkis and the Blood Moon phenomenon.

The Blood Moon Party

The Guardians attend Andrew Overdark’s Blood Moon Festival — until Bloodborn Husks rise.

The Blood Moon’s Light

Marcel Toxin investigates the Blood Moon and discovers the symbol of the Fogborn Syndicate.

The Blood Moon Memorial

A three‑chapter story exploring Andrew OverDark’s memories of his wife Lydia Lasil, his mourning under the Blood Moon, and the moment Seron contacts him with devastating news — Varkuun is under attack, and a cache of Crimson Fear Swords has been stolen.

Saga 3: The Varkuun Incident

The third major saga, following the aftermath of the attack on Varkuun. The Guardians align with Princess Anuket to recover the stolen Crimson Fear Arsenal before the Vexx Empire unlocks its destructive potential.

The Varkuun Incident — Chapters 1–5

The Guardians investigate the astral ruins of Varkuun, meeting Princess Anuket Nebet-Hut and discovering that Ral Awakening wields a genuine Crimson Fear Sword.

The Varkuun Incident — Chapters 6–10

Angelo Pierce arrives with pre-planned contingencies, neutralizes Vexx Administrator Rick Astro, and coordinates with Princess Anuket to track down the missing arsenal.

Beneath The Stormy Seas — Chapters 1–5

Ral Awakening and Angelo Pierce breach a Vexx Empire Dreadnought alone, brutally executing the Vexx garrison, dismantling their black-market smuggling operation, and securing the stolen Crimson Fear Arsenal while Princess Anuket watches in admiration.

Beneath The Stormy Seas — Chapters 6–10

Andrew OverDark arrives on Varkuun to aid the Guardians. Facing Director Ami Zero and the Vexx Empire, Andrew brutally neutralizes Ami while Ral unveils a deadly sword-and-chain combo that leaves Princess Anuket completely mesmerized.

Done The Impossible — Chapters 1–5 (Saga 3 Finale)

Seron Darkness arrives to sign off on the cleanup of Varkuun. Anuket asks Ral to date her, Seron invites Anuket to live on Guardia with Ral, and Tom receives a chilling alert from Marcel Toxin regarding a major Fogborn Syndicate revelation.

Saga 4: The Universal War Saga

The fourth major saga, documenting the complete conflict against Destrov, The Universal Ender. Armed with the Universal Ender Gauntlet, Destrov seeks to wipe out the universe and rebuild it in his own cold image—until he encounters the first Time Breaker God on OverDarkis.

The Universal War — Chapters 1–20 (Complete Saga)

Connor Violet and Casey Vigor encounter Destrov on Vor'kun as he claims the Universal Ender Gauntlet. After Seron rallies the Guardians on the Zavila Galaxy Asteroid and an intercept team clashes on Ignis Vortem, Destrov travels to OverDarkis for the final artifact—only to be brutally crushed by Andrew OverDark.

Saga 5: The Fogborn Syndicate Saga

The fifth major saga, documenting the complete campaign against Cindy Kavlor and her network of shadow wraiths across Guardia, Zavara, and Kacia.

The Fogborn Syndicate Saga (Complete Story)

A massive 20-chapter saga detailing the rise and fall of the Fogborn Syndicate. Follow the Guardians as Cindy Kavlor unleashes the Grey Veil across Guardia City, executes the Silent Census, deploys the Desert Echo Protocol in Kacia, activates the Banshee's Ledger to erase citizens from history, and launches the global unmaking broadcast—until Seron Darkness and the core Guardians storm the center of the Veil to shatter the Ledger once and for all.

Saga 6: The Black Prism Saga

The sixth major saga, detailing the sudden resurgence of the Black Prism faction across Zavara, Black Pine, and Night Hunter City, culminating in an off-world alliance war on Prismosa.

The Black Prism Saga (Complete Story)

A 7-act campaign tracking the return of the Black Prism faction. Follow Tom Letanio and Kole Rogue as anomalies erupt in the Zavara Mines and Black Pine, exposing a widespread execution list targeting the Guardians and Nova Watch. As an interstellar alliance forms, Seron Darkness and Angelo Darkness lead the assault on Prismosa, confronting the corrupted Emperor Spectra Vivida, disabling his stolen Vexx Armor, and liberating the Prismians from lingering Vexx control.

Series 1 — The Zavila Galaxy Series 1

The origin season of the Zavila Galaxy. Features 11 major stories, character awakenings, cosmic threats, and the first full-scale war of Guardia.

Series 2 — The Guardians Rising Era

The second major season of the Zavila Galaxy saga. Follows the aftermath of the Spike Empire’s fall, the rise of new threats, and the Guardians’ transition into galactic peacekeepers.

The Zavila Galaxy Series 3

The Hell Era — a multi‑dimensional war spanning Pandemonium, Zavara, and Guardians HQ. Featuring Tom Letanio’s Zenith Form, the rise of Lord Movkos, and the sealing of the Infernal Dimensions.

The Civil War — Chapter 1: The War Unnoticed

Casey Vigor’s powers malfunction, Nova Watch approaches him with lies, and Seron declares a Civil War after a confrontation at the Vexx Empire Crash Site.

The Zavara Heist

Seron Darkness, Tom Letanio, and Cody Kritz hunt Arsenio Beau Innes through abandoned Zavaran mines, uncovering the truth behind his past and his planned heist on the Zavaran Power Plant.

The Zavila Galaxy Series 4

The Chronos & Ignis Era — a dual‑saga exploring temporal collapse, ancient flame entities, and the rise of Babylon beneath Ignis Vortem.

Casey Vigor: The Dunes Of Time

Casey Vigor becomes stranded in the Kacia Desert and uncovers a buried Vexx Empire research station attempting to replicate Emperor Void’s corruption.

The Prism Order

The Zavara Heist Part 2 — Seron, Tom, and Cody uncover a Prism Order lab experimenting on civilians, revealing a conspiracy involving the Vexx Empire and rogue Guardian members.

The Diary Of Angelo

The early journals of Angelo Pierce — the Universal Starline Captain — chronicling his battles, discoveries, and the story of how Zavara became his home long before he met Seron or the Guardians.

Black Star Station

In 2036, Seron creates the Guardians’ first space‑station HQ — inspired by Angelo Pierce — a massive orbital fortress powered by Dark Star Essence.

Reach For The Stars

Tom Letanio uncovers a Prism Order lab in the Kacia Desert and discovers the truth — Vlasta Jeronim is the traitor within the Guardians.

The Blood Moon Party

Tom, Seron, and Kole attend the first Blood Moon Festival on OverDarkis — until Bloodborn Husks attack, powered by a Blood Moon Crystal that Tom destroys.

The Blood Moon's Light

Marcel Toxin investigates the Blood Moon on OverDarkis and discovers a second Blood Moon Crystal — and the ancient symbol of the Fogborn Syndicate.

Under Your Knife

The finale of the Zavara Heist storyline — Tom hunts down Vlasta Jeronim in the Aether Mines and ends the Prism Order once and for all.

The Blood Moon Memorial

Andrew OverDark revisits Lydia Lasil’s memorial beneath the Blood Moon, reliving their memories and confronting his grief. Seron reaches out through an Aether link, revealing that Varkuun is under siege and the Crimson Fear arsenal has been stolen — pulling Andrew back into the conflict.

The Varkuun Incident — Chapters 1–5

The Guardians arrive on Varkuun after Seron's warning, discovering the astral ruins and meeting Princess Anuket, who notices Ral's Crimson Fear Sword.

The Varkuun Incident — Chapters 6–10

Angelo Pierce arrives with active contingencies, detects the Vexx Empire, eliminates Administrator Rick Astro, and assesses the stolen Crimson Fear count with Princess Anuket.

Beneath The Stormy Seas — Chapters 1–5

Ral Awakening and Angelo Pierce breach a Vexx Empire Dreadnought, annihilating the forces inside, dismantling the black-market trade network, and recovering the stolen Crimson Fear Arsenal.

Beneath The Stormy Seas — Chapters 6–10

Andrew OverDark arrives on Varkuun to aid the Guardians. Facing Director Ami Zero and the Vexx Empire, Andrew brutally neutralizes Ami while Ral unveils a deadly sword-and-chain combo that leaves Princess Anuket completely mesmerized.
