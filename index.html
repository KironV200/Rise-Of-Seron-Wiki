Welcome to The Rise Of Seron

The Rise Of Seron Wiki is the central archive following the adventures, origins, and stories of Seron “Super” Darkness and The Guardians across multiple story arcs and sagas.

The Origin & Legacy of Seron

Seron did not begin merely as a warrior—he stands as an omniversal force. Bound to a legacy of controlled darkness, Seron acts as the prime defender of reality alongside his elite allies. His journeys span countless battles, uncovering ancient secrets, facing rogue factions, and preserving balance across space and time.

The Zavila Galaxy & Worlds

The Zavila Galaxy is the home galaxy of the Guardians, containing their primary strongholds, allied planets, and surrounding locations:

• Guardia — The home planet of the Guardians.
• Zavara — The sister planet of Guardia and the primary workforce powering its operations.
• OverDarkis — The blood moon planet and the home world of Andrew OverDark.
• Varkuun — A bounty hunter planet and the home of the Crimson Fears.
• Ignis Vortem — A volcanic lava planet.
• Draygon — A notorious bounty hunter world.
• Aurelis Prime — A high-tech city world situated right next to Vor'Kar.
• Vor'Kar — A harsh desert planet.
• Nirvana-Xenthos — A frozen snow planet distinctively carved by red rivers.

The Guardians

To defend the Zavila Galaxy and all surrounding sectors, Seron leads The Guardians—an elite team of warriors, tacticians, and cosmic protectors. Together, they confront galaxy-spanning threats, investigate rogue factions, and uphold justice across every world and location within their realm.

Their key responsibilities include:

• Protecting Guardia and the Zavila Galaxy from hostile forces
• Investigating dark anomalies, ancient artifacts, and dangerous technologies
• Standing as the front line against rogue syndicates and dark conspiracies
• Maintaining order across all allied planets, bounty territories, and dangerous frontiers
