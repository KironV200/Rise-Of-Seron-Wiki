Characters

Seron “Super” Darkness

Seron is the primordial architect of the universe — a former human who ascended after surviving the collapse of his original reality. Absorbed into a dying Dark Star, he awakened as a cosmic entity capable of shaping galaxies, dimensions, and the laws of existence itself.

Calm, distant, and impossibly powerful, Seron serves as the silent stabilizing force behind the Zavila Galaxy. His influence is woven into every star and every event, even when he does not appear directly.

Kole Rogue

Kole Rogue — known throughout the Zavila Galaxy as The Arsonist — is one of the earliest and most unstable Seron-Type clones ever created. Born from an experimental attempt to replicate Seron’s cosmic structure, Kole inherited only fragments of Seron’s power, but those fragments manifested as uncontrollable thermal energy and violent emotional surges.

Kole fights with a molten fire axe capable of melting steel and igniting entire battlefields. His body constantly radiates heat, and his presence alone can warp the air around him. Though aggressive, reckless, and driven by instinct, Kole is not inherently evil — he is a fractured being struggling to understand his identity and purpose.

His unpredictable nature makes him a dangerous wildcard in the galaxy. Sometimes an enemy, sometimes an ally, and sometimes a force of nature acting on pure emotion, Kole’s actions often shift the balance of major conflicts. His full story, abilities, and origins are detailed on his dedicated page.

Angelis “Shifter” Darkness

Angelis is a Seron‑type clone who rejected cosmic destiny and instead became the galaxy’s most feared street‑level hunter. Born without Seron’s cosmic abilities, Angelis developed heightened reflexes, brutal efficiency, and a predatory instinct that earned him the title The Night Hunter.

Operating in the darkest corners of the Zavila Galaxy, Angelis hunts criminals, corrupt officials, and violent offenders using a SPAS‑12 shotgun, a Desert Eagle, dual pistols, and his signature crowbar. His combat style is ruthless, fast, and grounded — the opposite of Seron’s cosmic grace.

Cold, intense, and morally uncompromising, Angelis is the shadow Seron never wanted to become — but sometimes needed. He is the clone who fights the battles no one else sees, protecting the innocent through fear, precision, and relentless pursuit.

Angelo Pierce

Angelo is a legendary space‑faring tactician and former commander of the Universal Starline. His flagship vanished during a catastrophic battle, only to be rediscovered decades later — intact when it should have been destroyed. Angelo’s knowledge of cosmic warfare, starship systems, and contingency planning makes him an invaluable ally to Seron.

He is known for creating contingency plans for every Guardian, not out of distrust, but out of respect for the destructive potential they carry. Angelo’s return marks the beginning of a new era in the ROS Universe.

Mason “Killer” Darkness

Mason is a Xanian‑born Guardian whose raw physical power rivals early cosmic titans. Nicknamed “Killer” during the Fracture Era, Mason earned his reputation by single‑handedly eliminating void‑spawned monstrosities that threatened the Zavila Galaxy.

Though feared by enemies, Mason is fiercely loyal to Seron and the Guardians. His Xanian biology grants him enhanced regeneration, extreme durability, and the ability to channel destructive kinetic bursts. Mason is a frontline warrior who thrives in impossible battles.

HawkBat Rogue

HawkBat Rogue — known universally as The Scarlet Elemental — is one of the earliest stable Seron-type clones, created from Seron Darkness’s heroic essence rather than his cosmic or destructive traits. This unique imprint produced a clone with exceptional emotional stability, protective instinct, and scarlet-aether mutation.

HawkBat’s Scarlet Aether allows him to generate crimson flame, scarlet constructs, and high-intensity elemental bursts capable of overwhelming corrupted entities. Calm, disciplined, and fiercely loyal, HawkBat serves as one of the Rogue Family’s strongest heroic pillars and acts as a stabilizing force for his brothers Kole, Michael, and Dominic.

Michael Rogue

Michael Rogue is the most emotionally stable of the Seron‑type clones — a rare creation whose identity formed around compassion, restraint, and clarity rather than chaos. While he lacks Seron’s cosmic scale, he possesses something equally valuable: a balanced mind capable of wielding temporal power without corruption.

Michael carries two signature tools: a stolen temporal pocket watch once owned by the tyrant Grayson Talon, and a guitar‑axe that channels harmonic energy into shockwaves and stabilizing fields. These abilities make him one of the most versatile Guardians in the Zavila Galaxy.

Calm, empathetic, and morally grounded, Michael often acts as the emotional anchor among the clones. He is the one who chooses understanding over domination, and time over chaos — a stabilizing force in a universe shaped by conflict.

Andrew OverDark

Andrew OverDark — known as Black Rose — is one of the most feared temporal beings in existence. Once an ordinary man, Andrew awakened catastrophic time‑breaking power after witnessing the murder of his wife during the Angelic Purges.

Consumed by grief and sleep‑deprived fury, Andrew hunted the angelic host across timelines, erasing their leader Adam from existence. He is not evil — but he is merciless, a god forged from heartbreak whose power reshaped cosmic history.

Riven Solari

Riven Solari is a half‑angel warrior who defected from the celestial regime after uncovering the truth behind their purges. His hybrid nature grants him immense physical power, but his true weapon is Emperor Red, a temporal Stand capable of erasing or skipping segments of time.

Calm, disciplined, and morally grounded, Riven fights not for revenge but for justice. His knowledge of angelic warfare and his temporal abilities make him one of the most unpredictable allies in the Zavila Galaxy.

David Spike

Once Seron’s closest friend, David Spike was consumed by a collapsing star‑void that rewrote his memories and twisted his body into a vessel of entropy. Now known as The Black Nebula, David wields destructive entropy energy capable of decaying matter, destabilizing regions, and tearing through cosmic barriers.

His corruption is fueled by a lie — the belief that Seron abandoned him. This false memory drives his rage, making him one of the most tragic and catastrophic threats in the ROS Universe.

Connor Violet

Connor Violet, known as Captain Violet, is a Serlium‑enhanced warrior who fights with a blue cyber revolver and the legendary Serlium Shield — a near‑indestructible artifact capable of absorbing cosmic, elemental, and temporal attacks.

Tactical, loyal, and unwaveringly brave, Connor stands as one of Seron’s most trusted allies. His presence on the battlefield is a symbol of stability and hope across the Zavila Galaxy.

Ral Awakening

Ral Awakening is a fear‑driven executioner whose trauma‑born awakening granted him terrifying abilities. Wielding Crimson Fear — a blade that amplifies terror — and reinforced chains, Ral fights with a brutal, unpredictable hybrid style.

Cold, unstable, and sadistic, Ral spreads psychological devastation wherever he appears. He is one of the most dangerous independent threats in the ROS Universe.

Casey Vigor

Casey Vigor is the last survivor of the Vigor bloodline — a family once known for their rare isotope‑based abilities. After witnessing the extermination of his lineage, Casey underwent Isotope Ascension, transforming into an immortal nuclear entity.

Calm, patient, and quietly powerful, Casey channels his trauma into purpose. His nuclear abilities make him a stabilizing force in cosmic conflicts and a symbol of survival across the Zavila Galaxy.

Marcel Toxin

Marcel Toxin is a biochemical genius whose own experiments reshaped his body. A catastrophic lab accident altered his lungs and nerves, forcing him to breathe toxin‑filtered air and leaving him unable to feel physical touch.

Wielding a green bio‑energy scythe and a mind sharpened by scientific obsession, Marcel treats the battlefield like a laboratory. Detached but not heartless, he walks the line between hero and hazard.

Nathan Ice

Nathan Ice is the calm and disciplined King of the Iceberg Tower — a frozen fortress in the far north of the Zavila Galaxy. As an Ice Elemental Ascendant, Nathan commands glacial energy, sub‑zero temperatures, and crystalline constructs with unmatched precision.

Forced into leadership after the Angelic Purges claimed his parents, Nathan grew into a protective and strategic monarch. His power is fueled not by rage, but by duty — making him one of the galaxy’s most stable and respected elemental rulers.

Samuel “Wave” Andreas

Wave is Guardia City’s beloved Comedian Hero — a fast‑talking, joke‑cracking fighter whose humor is as iconic as his abilities. Born with a rare adaptive mimic mutation, Wave can instantly copy the powers of anyone he sees, making him one of the most unpredictable heroes in the galaxy.

His jokes aren’t just for show — they keep allies calm, throw enemies off rhythm, and turn fear into confidence. Beneath the humor is a compassionate hero who refuses to let darkness win.

Tom Letanio

Tom Letanio is a disciplined temporal guardian who wields Indigo Fist — a powerful close‑range Stand capable of stopping time indefinitely. Alongside his Stand, Tom commands the Blue Phoenix, a celestial spirit of rebirth and blue flame.

Traumatized by the loss of his parents during the Angelic Purges, Tom forged himself into a calm, controlled protector. His mastery of time, phoenix fire, and guardian discipline makes him one of the most reliable defenders in the Zavila Galaxy.

Grayson Talon

Grayson Talon, known across the galaxy as The Pocket Watch Tyrant, is a brilliant but unstable temporal engineer whose experiments shattered his mind into multiple elemental personalities. Grayson created the legendary Temporal Pocket Watch, the same artifact later stolen and used by Michael Rogue.

Once a respected chronomancer, Grayson pushed too far into forbidden time‑fracture research. The backlash split his consciousness into alternate personas — each with its own elemental affinity and combat style. Grayson himself remains the cold, calculating core personality, obsessed with reclaiming his lost inventions and restoring his fractured identity.

Ignis Blaze

Ignis Blaze is the volatile fire‑elemental personality born from Grayson Talon’s fractured mind. Unlike Grayson’s cold precision, Ignis is pure instinct — a living inferno driven by emotion, destruction, and raw heat.

Ignis manifests whenever Grayson’s rage overwhelms his control. In this form, he wields Pyro‑Flux, a burning aura capable of melting steel, igniting air, and overwhelming opponents with explosive flame bursts. Ignis is unpredictable, aggressive, and nearly impossible to reason with — the embodiment of Grayson’s suppressed fury.

Finn Sealot

Finn Sealot is the Solaris‑aligned personality created when Grayson Talon’s mind fractured under temporal overload. Calm, radiant, and eerily serene, Finn represents Grayson’s idealized self — the version of him untouched by corruption or obsession.

Finn commands Solaris Energy, channeling concentrated sunlight into beams, shields, and purifying blasts capable of cleansing corrupted matter. Unlike Ignis, Finn is peaceful and philosophical, often appearing to guide or stabilize Grayson’s collapsing psyche. But when threatened, his Solaris power becomes one of the most destructive forces in the galaxy.

Esteban Maverick

Known across the coastlines as The Gunslinger of Beach City, Esteban Maverick is a fast‑talking, sharp‑shooting Guardian whose confidence borders on arrogance — and he earns every bit of it. His dual pistols, Sunrise and High Tide, channel kinetic Aether rounds capable of piercing armor and destabilizing corrupted entities.

Esteban joined Team Crusader after saving an entire district during the Angelic Purges, and Seron personally recruited him into the Guardians for his precision, adaptability, and fearless attitude. Beneath the cocky exterior, Esteban is fiercely loyal and hides a strategic mind sharper than his aim.

Royal Demetrios

Royal Demetrios is the 2nd Time Breaker God — a being capable of bending temporal flow, rewriting micro‑timelines, and freezing causality itself. As the Leader of Team Crusader, Royal carries the weight of countless timelines on his shoulders, leaving him serious, calculating, and perpetually tired.

Despite his exhaustion, Royal remains one of the most reliable Guardians in existence. His mastery of Chrono‑Aether allows him to predict enemy movements, counter multiversal threats, and stabilize fractured dimensions. Royal rarely smiles, but when he does, it’s usually because someone finally impressed him.

Marlo Laston (Silver Fang)

Marlo Laston — known across the Travia Arctic as Silver Fang — is a mercenary whose cold‑born instincts and razor precision make him one of the deadliest hunters in the outer rim. Raised in blizzards and warzones, Marlo learned to track, stalk, and eliminate targets with silent efficiency.

Though independent, Marlo frequently assists Angelis “Shifter” Darkness during high‑risk hunts. His frost‑enhanced reflexes, arctic survival training, and silver‑edge combat claws make him a perfect partner for the Night Hunter.

Mindy Valor

Mindy Valor is the strategic and disciplined leader of Nova Watch, known throughout the galaxy as The Watcher. Her observational abilities allow her to analyze Aether signatures, predict enemy movements, and track anomalies across entire star systems.

Once Seron’s partner, Mindy still carries deep care for him despite their separation. Her leadership, intelligence, and emotional restraint make her one of the most respected commanders in the Zavila Galaxy.

Verna Corinne

Verna Corinne — known as The Ascended Ember — is one of the newest members of Team Crusader. Born with a rare Ember‑Core mutation, Verna can ignite her own life‑force into blazing ascension flame, amplifying strength, speed, and perception beyond Guardian standards.

Calm, radiant, and fiercely loyal, Verna serves as the emotional stabilizer of the Crusaders. Her Ember Ascension allows her to burn through corruption, ignite Aether pathways, and overwhelm cosmic threats with pure flame‑born willpower.

Jenny Dexly

Jenny Dexly is a powerful Scarlet Elemental whose emotional resonance fuels her crimson‑aether abilities. Once Seron’s partner, Jenny separated from him after the Angelic Purges, choosing independence over cosmic destiny.

Her Scarlet Core grants her explosive elemental bursts, crimson constructs, and emotional amplification that can destabilize corrupted entities. Jenny remains one of the most influential elemental figures in the Zavila Galaxy.

Kyler JadeBlade

Kyler JadeBlade — known as The Temporal Swordsman — is one of the newest recruits of Team Crusader. Wielding the Jade‑Edge Chronoblade, Kyler can slice through micro‑timelines, delay impacts, and rewrite short‑range causality.

Disciplined, quiet, and razor‑focused, Kyler fights with surgical precision. His temporal swordsmanship makes him one of the most dangerous close‑quarters fighters in the Crusader roster.

Dominic Rogue

Dominic Rogue is the brother of Michael, Kole, HawkBat Rogue, Angelis Darkness, Angelo Pierce, and Miles Church — a Seron‑type clone forged with enhanced stability and combat instinct. Like his brothers, Dominic carries fragments of Seron’s cosmic structure.

Dominic is a Stand User, wielding Crimson Vanguard — a close‑range Stand specializing in rapid strikes, temporal feints, and crimson‑energy shockwaves. Calm, loyal, and battle‑ready, Dominic is one of the most reliable fighters among the Seron‑type lineage.

Peter Hydros

Peter Hydros — widely known as Electro Boy — is a Guardian whose pure electric abilities make him the livewire of Team Crusader. His personality is a chaotic blend of humor, goofy confidence, and sudden poetic wisdom that appears at the most unexpected moments.

Peter generates and manipulates raw electricity, allowing him to unleash lightning bursts, charge his body with voltage, accelerate his movements, and overload enemy systems. Despite his comedic nature, Peter becomes sharply serious when the situation demands it — especially when protecting Royal and Esteban, whom he considers his closest brothers.

Marty Crispin

Marty Crispin is a Prismosa‑born explorer who wields the legendary Aether Dial — a device that grants him the powers of countless species across the Zavila Galaxy. Each form enhances his abilities without altering his physical appearance, making him unpredictable and extremely versatile.

Marty’s adaptability, curiosity, and cosmic knowledge make him a valuable ally in multi‑species conflicts and unknown‑world expeditions.

Proto Ballos

Proto Ballos is the Second Leader of the Universal Starline and one of Angelo Pierce’s closest friends. Unlike most Starline officers, Proto is not a clone or enhanced soldier — he is a fully sentient Aetherian built through experimental Starline engineering, designed for leadership, strategy, and frontline combat.

Proto wields a green laser pistol with near‑perfect aim accuracy, capable of firing condensed emerald beams that pierce armor and destabilize corrupted energy. His precision is legendary across the Zavila Galaxy, often landing shots at impossible ranges.

Brave, charismatic, and fiercely loyal to Angelo, Proto Ballos serves as the Starline’s stabilizing force — the commander who keeps the crew united, disciplined, and battle‑ready.

Arin Valtrex

Arin Valtrex is a frontline Universal Starline fighter who channels Azure Pulse Energy through his fists. His combat style blends martial arts with explosive blue‑aether bursts, allowing him to shatter armor and disrupt corrupted constructs with raw force.

Loyal, loud, and fearless, Arin is one of the Starline’s most aggressive close‑quarters fighters — a living engine of blue energy who never backs down from a challenge.

Carl Levi Van Baarle

Carl Levi Van Baarle is a Guardian who commands Sand Aether, manipulating dunes, grit, and desert winds with precision. His abilities allow him to create sandstorms, hardened sand‑armor, and razor‑sharp sand blades.

Calm, strategic, and deeply connected to the Kacia Desert, Carl serves as one of the Guardians’ most reliable elemental defenders.

Launo Séafra

Launo Séafra is a legendary sword master from the Outerlands of Zavara. Trained in ancient blade‑arts, Launo wields a forged Outerlands steelblade capable of channeling kinetic resonance through each strike.

Stoic, disciplined, and feared across Zavara’s frontier, Launo stands as one of the most skilled swordsmen in the Zavila Galaxy.

Lean Antonia

Lean Antonia is the Universal Starline Sergeant, a blind warrior whose heightened senses allow her to perceive vibrations, footsteps, and energy shifts with perfect clarity.

She wields a precision crossbow, landing flawless shots without sight — guided entirely by her sensory awareness and battlefield intuition.

Mason Walker

Mason Walker is a disciplined member of the Black Prism, wielding a green energy spear forged from prism‑aether. His spear can extend, split, and emit concentrated energy bursts.

Focused and tactical, Mason serves as one of the Black Prism’s elite spear‑fighters.

Patsy Leon

Patsy Leon is a famous basketball player and rapper who openly supports Nova Watch. Known for his loud personality and anti‑Guardian diss tracks, Patsy uses his influence to rally public support for Nova Watch’s ideology.

Charismatic, flashy, and unapologetic, Patsy is one of the most recognizable public figures in the Zavila Galaxy.

Viato Kulmala

Viato Kulmala is a disciplined member of the Shadow Samurai Clan and the nephew of Kauko Roni Kulmala. He wields void‑mist blades and shadow‑step techniques passed down through the BlackFlower lineage.

Quiet, focused, and deadly, Viato represents the next generation of Shadow Samurai warriors.

Milo Kulmala

Milo Kulmala is Kauko’s cousin and a trained Shadow Samurai specializing in stealth‑based void combat. His techniques emphasize silent movement, shadow‑cuts, and rapid‑strike assassination forms.

Loyal to the BlackFlower Clan, Milo serves as one of its most efficient covert operatives.

Ebony Marcoto

Ebony Marcoto is the calculating Director of the Vexx Empire — a genetically engineered smart‑clone of Seron created to replicate his intelligence without his cosmic power. Ebony possesses hyper‑analysis, strategic foresight, and near‑perfect emotional control.

Cold, brilliant, and quietly ambitious, Ebony directs Vexx operations with surgical efficiency. Though created as a weapon, Ebony seeks autonomy and secretly questions the Empire’s motives.

Arthur Forest

Arthur Forest is a Time Breaker Gunslinger whose dual chrono‑revolvers, Forest Dawn and Timberfall, fire temporal‑charged rounds capable of freezing, skipping, or accelerating localized time.

As one of the newest members of Team Crusader, Arthur brings calm precision, frontier discipline, and a unique blend of gunslinger instinct and temporal mastery.

Sobek Anapa Awakening

Sobek Anapa Awakening is the son of Ral Awakening and Princess Anuket Nebet‑Hut. Born with hybrid fear‑aether and astral‑royal physiology, Sobek wields both Crimson Fear resonance and Varkuun astral combat arts.

Unpredictable, intense, and fiercely protective, Sobek is a rising force whose hybrid nature makes him one of the most dangerous young warriors in the galaxy.

Venus Rogue

Venus Rogue is the alternate personality of Michael Rogue, manifested from the corrupted temporal pocket watch once owned by Grayson Talon. Venus represents Michael’s suppressed instincts and fractured time‑echoes, forming a separate temporal entity with his own consciousness.

Sharp‑minded, composed, and dangerously perceptive, Venus manipulates harmonic‑time fields and emotional resonance with precision. As a temporal anomaly within the Rogue lineage, he stands as one of the most unpredictable and powerful manifestations tied to the Rogue family’s chronal heritage.

Petros Quinn Rogue

Petros Quinn Rogue is a Seron‑type clone engineered by the Vexx Empire to replicate the Rogue Family’s cloning lineage. Petros escaped Vexx control and became a space‑pirate scavenger known as The Astral Monarch.

Petros commands astral‑drift energy, allowing him to warp through star‑dust currents, forge astral weapons, and navigate cosmic ruins with unmatched instinct. A rebel, a survivor, and a monarch of the astral frontier.

Enrico Liberatore

Enrico Liberatore is the son of Angelis “Shifter” Darkness and Vlasta Jeronim — a former Guardian secretly aligned with the Prism Order of Prismosa.

Enrico inherited Shifter’s predatory instinct and Vlasta’s prism‑aether mutation, granting him multi‑spectrum combat abilities, refracted perception, and adaptive elemental resonance. Enrico is a rising prodigy whose hybrid nature makes him one of the most versatile young fighters in the galaxy.

Aurora Lumina

Aurora Lumina is a rare Black Aetherian, born from the remnants of Project: Gemini and awakened with adaptive Black Aether that responds directly to her emotions. Gentle, observant, and deeply empathetic, Aurora’s presence stabilizes corrupted energy and calms volatile Aether fields.

Aurora is the current girlfriend of Peter Hydros, forming one of the strongest emotional bonds in the Guardians. Her Black Aether manifests as void‑snow, eclipse bursts, and radiant heartflare ascension when she feels hope or love.

Ivan BlackWood

Ivan BlackWood — known as ShadowStrike — is one of the sons of Kauko Roni Kulmala, inheriting the ancient Urai shadow arts of the Shadow Samurai. Ivan channels razor‑sharp void mist, shadow cuts, and stealth‑based combat techniques refined through BlackFlower Clan tradition.

Focused, disciplined, and fiercely loyal to his family, Ivan stands as one of the strongest Shadow Samurai descendants, wielding darkness with precision and honor.

Blade BlackWood

Blade BlackWood is the official Shadow Samurai Heir, chosen by Kauko to inherit the full legacy of the BlackFlower Clan. Blade’s mastery of shadow energy surpasses most modern practitioners, allowing him to phase through attacks, cloak himself in void armor, and unleash devastating shadow‑cuts.

Stoic, strategic, and unwavering, Blade represents the future of the Shadow Samurai lineage and carries the responsibility of preserving its ancient traditions.

Keith Mystery

Keith Mystery is a gifted Spirit User bonded with a protective female spirit named Cyan Piercer. Cyan manifests as a spectral guardian capable of forming spirit‑weapons, barriers, and piercing constructs that respond to Keith’s emotional state.

Keith serves as the personal bodyguard of Dr. Anthony Red, using his spirit abilities to shield Red Corp operations from cosmic threats. Calm, loyal, and perceptive, Keith is one of the most reliable protectors in Zavara.

Anthony Red (Dr. Red)

Anthony Red — widely known as Dr. Red — is a former scientist of the Nova Watch organization who later founded Red Corp Industries, a technological powerhouse specializing in medical innovation, Aether research, and advanced weaponry.

Brilliant, eccentric, and morally flexible, Dr. Red pushes scientific boundaries to protect Zavara from cosmic threats. His inventions are used by Guardians, Crusaders, and independent factions across the galaxy.

Jotaro Kanjo

Jotaro Kanjo is a powerful Stand User who commands the cosmic stand Star Requiem — a manifestation of astral willpower capable of delivering reality‑breaking strikes, temporal distortions, and star‑core resonance attacks.

As a Guardian Enforcer, Jotaro operates on the front lines of cosmic conflict, enforcing Guardian law with unmatched precision. His stand makes him one of the most dangerous close‑quarters fighters in the galaxy.

Ovide Jem Clifton

Ovide Jem Clifton is a ruthless Stand User aligned with Nova Watch, wielding the spectral stand Ghostly Hollow. His stand manifests as a hollow phantom capable of draining vitality, phasing through matter, and unleashing echo‑based shockwaves.

Cold, calculating, and openly hostile toward Team Crusader, Ovide views the Crusaders as naive obstacles to Nova Watch’s “necessary evolution.” His Ghostly Hollow makes him one of the most dangerous stand users in the organization.

Spectra Vivida

Spectra Vivida is the radiant Empress of Prismosa, ruling the prism‑born kingdom with unmatched elegance and cosmic authority. Her Prism Aether allows her to refract energy, manipulate multi‑spectrum light, and generate prismatic constructs of immense power.

Wise, diplomatic, and fiercely protective of her people, Spectra stands as one of the galaxy’s most influential leaders — a beacon of harmony in an era of chaos.

Valco Kieve

Valco Kieve is Nova Watch’s elite Spectral Swordsman, wielding a ghost‑forged blade capable of slicing through matter, energy, and even weakened timelines. His spectral techniques allow him to phase, vanish, and strike with supernatural precision.

Silent, disciplined, and merciless, Valco serves as one of Nova Watch’s deadliest enforcers, executing missions with spectral efficiency.

William Radioa

William Radioa — known as The Annihilator Battery — is a powerful radioactive Guardian whose body generates controlled nuclear energy. His Radioactive Aether allows him to unleash annihilation bursts, radiation shields, and atomic propulsion.

Despite his destructive potential, William is calm, analytical, and deeply loyal to the Guardians, serving as one of their most reliable heavy‑class enforcers.

Lora Kavil

Lora Kavil is a dangerously obsessive Vexx Empire officer who developed an intense yandere fixation on Peter Hydros after witnessing his Hydros Ascension during a Vexx raid. Her devotion is extreme, unstable, and often violent.

Lora’s Vexx Aether grants her enhanced agility, predatory instinct, and emotional amplification, making her a volatile threat driven by twisted affection.

Jenna Vigor

Jenna Vigor is the wealthy and internationally famous supermodel and business mogul of Guardia — the face of Vigor Industries and one of the most influential public figures in the Zavila Galaxy. Believed to be the last surviving member of the Vigor bloodline, Jenna built her empire from tragedy, convinced her brother Casey died during the Vexx Empire invasion.

Elegant, disciplined, and fiercely independent, Jenna commands fashion empires, charity networks, and high-end tech brands. Her life changes forever when she uncovers evidence that Casey is still alive — a revelation that shatters her world and reignites the Vigor legacy.

Cindy Kavlor

Cindy Kavlor — feared as The Banshee — is the ruthless leader of the Fogborn Syndicate, a criminal network operating through mist‑based stealth and spectral infiltration. Her Banshee Aether allows her to emit sonic shrieks, fog illusions, and ghost‑mist constructs.

Cold, manipulative, and unpredictable, Cindy commands the Fogborn with spectral terror and unmatched cunning.

Eon Data

Eon Data is a highly advanced female humanoid AI created in Prismosa to manage multi‑spectrum archives, astral communication networks, and prism‑energy stabilization systems.

Logical, curious, and quietly compassionate, Eon Data serves as Spectra Vivida’s primary advisor and one of Prismosa’s most valuable technological assets.

Destrov

Destrov is a cosmic executioner known as The Universal Ender, a primordial entity forged to erase corrupted universes and reset broken timelines. His Ender Aether is capable of collapsing dimensions, nullifying cosmic energy, and extinguishing entire realities.

Emotionless, ancient, and unstoppable, Destrov exists beyond morality — a force of cosmic correction feared across the multiverse.

Dallas Red-Star

Dallas Red-Star is the legendary Kacia Desert Guardian, wielding sandstorm manipulation, desert‑aether resonance, and heat‑burst combat techniques. As a Guardian Enforcer, Dallas protects the Kacia frontier from cosmic threats.

Stoic, honorable, and fiercely protective of his homeland, Dallas stands as one of the most respected Guardians in the desert regions.

Adam Helios

Adam Helios is the ruthless commander of the Angelic Purges, wielding holy‑light aether capable of burning corruption, purifying matter, and annihilating cosmic anomalies.

Fanatical, disciplined, and unwavering in his mission, Adam believes the Purges are the only path to universal order — making him one of the most feared figures in the galaxy.

Jason Zachary

Jason Zachary is a rare Jinx Elemental, capable of manipulating luck, probability, and chaotic energy. His Jinx Aether allows him to twist outcomes, disrupt enemy abilities, and create unpredictable combat scenarios.

Energetic, mischievous, and dangerously clever, Jason is both a wildcard and a powerful ally in cosmic conflict.

Kvido Benjamin

Kvido Benjamin is a cosmic anomaly known as The Reality Rewriter, capable of altering physical laws, rewriting events, and bending reality to his will. His Rewriter Aether makes him one of the most unpredictable beings in existence.

Detached from normal perception, Kvido views reality as a canvas — and himself as its painter.

Chrok

Chrok is the ancient Kacia Desert Time Lord, wielding chronal sand manipulation and desert‑temporal resonance. His abilities allow him to freeze dunes, rewind desert storms, and accelerate time within localized sand fields.

Wise, mysterious, and deeply connected to the Kacia desert, Chrok serves as both protector and temporal guardian of the region.

Angel Kieve

Angel Kieve is the ruthless Spectral Swordswoman of the Vexx Empire, wielding corrupted spectral blades forged from void‑aether and emotional resonance. Once the girlfriend of Peter Hydros, Angel’s fall into the Vexx Empire reshaped her into a cold, disciplined, and merciless operative.

As the older sister of Valco Kieve, Angel shares the Kieve family’s natural spectral affinity — but her Vexx conditioning twisted her abilities into predatory, crimson‑tinted spectral constructs. Angel fights with precision, emotional suppression, and lethal efficiency, making her one of the Empire’s most feared swordswomen.

Mason Antelmo Vigor

Mason Antelmo Vigor is the seasoned uncle of Casey and Jenna Vigor — a former member of the Universal Starline Pirate Crew under Angelo Pierce. Once feared across cosmic trade routes for his mechanical brilliance and starship sabotage skills, Mason retired after the collapse of the Starline era.

Now living in Zavara, Mason works as a quiet but brilliant engineer, repairing starships, designing reactor cores, and secretly monitoring cosmic anomalies. Though gruff and world-weary, Mason carries deep love for his niece and nephew — and he is one of the few who suspects Casey may still be alive.

Samuel Stardust

Samuel Stardust is a supernatural hybrid created by the Vexx Empire through astral‑spectral mutation. Designed as a living weapon, Samuel was engineered to channel stardust particles, spectral energy, and cosmic vampirism. After escaping Vexx control, he joined the Guardians, becoming one of their most unpredictable but invaluable allies.

Samuel can summon spectral entities formed from astral echoes, manipulate stardust into weapons and barriers, and absorb cosmic energy through his vampiric mutation. Despite his dark origins, he is quiet, gentle, and deeply protective of others.

Kauko Roni Kulmala

Kauko Roni Kulmala is a Shadowborn Ascendant and the founder of the Shadow Samurai Dynasty. Born with Shadowborn Physiology, Kauko possesses a natural connection to elemental darkness, allowing him to merge with shadows, move silently, and strike with void‑enhanced precision.

He forged the BlackWood Citadel atop a dimensional rift and created the Shadow Samurai Order, training warriors to wield darkness with discipline and honor. Kauko commands shadows across entire regions, wields the Riftblade forged from the BlackWood rift, and moves through shadow rifts using Rift Step. Stoic, calculating, and unwavering, Kauko is one of the most powerful shadow‑combat figures in the Zavila Galaxy.

Lynne Esmaralda

Lynne Esmaralda is a Heartstalker Aetherian born in the forests of Guardia. Gifted with emotional‑tracking Aether, she can sense feelings like scents in the air and follow heroic emotional signatures across miles. Drawn naturally to strong heroic energy, Lynne left her Heartstalker community to follow the Guardians, whom she admires with overwhelming devotion.

Lynne boosts morale through her Heartbind Aura, can shadow‑step after those she bonds with, and gains sudden strength when someone she admires is in danger. Sweet, dramatic, excitable, and socially awkward, she tries her best to help the Guardians and has become a harmless, loyal companion who often warns them of emotional disturbances before they occur.

Michael Valentino

Michael Valentino is an elite Guardian Enforcer known for his cosmic‑temporal Stand, Stellaris. Capable of stopping time for several seconds, generating gravitational fields, and unleashing stellar shockwaves, Michael excels in close‑quarters suppression and high‑risk arrests. His Stand grants him cosmic awareness, allowing him to sense movement and energy shifts around him.
