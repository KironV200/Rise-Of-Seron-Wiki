The Complete Rise Of Seron Timeline

The Rise Of Seron timeline spans centuries of cosmic history across the Zavila Galaxy. From Seron’s birth and the destruction of his universe, to the Angelic Purges, the Void Rift, the rise of the Vexx Empire, the Universal War, and the final peace — this page chronicles every major event that shaped the Guardians and the galaxy itself.

Pre‑Beyond The Rift

  • Seron Darkness and Killer Darkness are born on the remnants of Seron’s original universe.
  • Seron’s universe collapses in a cosmic implosion, leaving him as the sole survivor.
  • Maria Darkness disappears during the collapse, becoming one of the galaxy’s oldest mysteries.
  • Seron awakens as The Dark Star, gaining cosmic-tier abilities.
  • Seron meets David Spike and Connor Violet; the three form an unbreakable brotherhood.
  • The Guardians are founded — a small team meant to protect Guardia from cosmic threats.
  • Seron, Connor, and David spend years partying, exploring planets, and bonding as brothers.
  • Tom Letanio, Casey Vigor, Ral Awakening, and others are born across Guardia and Zavara.
  • Andrew OverDark works as a young engineer on Zavara, designing early Aether reactors.
  • Andrew meets Lydia, who becomes his emotional anchor and future wife.
  • Seron meets Mindy Valor; they date and eventually have a son, David Kaine.
  • Adam Helios and his angelic army go rogue, beginning the First Angelic Purge.

The Angelic Purges

  • Andrew and Lydia move to OverDarkis, drawn by its beauty and untouched wilderness.
  • Heaven launches coordinated attacks on Guardia, Zavara, OverDarkis, and other worlds.
  • Andrew’s Aether awakens violently during the attack, unlocking his OverDark potential.
  • Tom’s Aether awakens after witnessing the destruction of his home.
  • Lute, Riven, and Vaggie betray Heaven, siding with the Guardians.
  • Lute rescues Tom and brings him to Seron for protection.
  • Andrew’s wife Lydia is killed by angels; Tom’s parents are also targeted and killed.
  • The Guardians ally with Hell; Lucifer agrees to help stop Heaven’s rogue army.
  • Andrew goes on a galaxy-wide rampage, killing angels to avenge Lydia.
  • Andrew brutally kills Adam Helios, ending the First Angelic Purge.
  • Riven, Seron, and Andrew confront Heaven; the Purges are permanently banned.

Beyond The Rift

  • A massive Void Rift opens on Guardia, destabilizing reality.
  • Seron, David, and Connor investigate the anomaly.
  • Void creatures swarm the area, forcing the Guardians into battle.
  • David Spike is grabbed by a Void tendril and dragged into the portal.
  • Seron is struck by a Void tendril, infecting him with Void Corruption.
  • Seron becomes quieter, colder, and emotionally fractured.
  • Seron breaks up with Mindy; Jenny Dexly enters his life.
  • Jenny becomes abusive and manipulative; Seron takes Persephone to protect her.
  • Seron’s fractured mind creates clones: Kole, Michael, HawkBat, Angelo, and others.
  • Seron adopts Ral Awakening and Casey Vigor, raising them as his own.

Post‑Beyond The Rift

  • Angelo Pierce forms the Universal Starline Crew, becoming a feared space pirate emperor.
  • Seron, Andrew, and others visit Earth and Beach City; they meet the Crystal Gems.
  • Some Crystal Gems return to Guardia with them, forming new relationships.
  • Ral, Tom, Casey, and others reach adulthood due to Aetherian fast-aging.
  • The Vexx Empire is created by Angelo Pierce; he later leaves to reunite with Seron.
  • Tom meets Verosika, I.M.P, Lucifer, and Hazbin Hotel; he becomes King Indigo.
  • New members join The Guardians: Nathan, Dallas, and others.
  • Killer Darkness reunites with Seron and joins The Guardians.
  • Team Crusader joins the Guardian forces.
  • The Vexx Empire begins creating Artificial Aetherians.
  • David Spike returns from the Void; he fights Seron in a catastrophic battle.
  • Miles Church heals David, restoring his sanity.
  • David rejoins The Guardians.
  • Michael Rogue discovers The Pocket Watch and meets Grayson Talon, Ignis Blaze, Finn Sealot.
  • Michael learns the Pocket Watch is sentient — containing Hina Cleopatra.
  • Venus Rogue is born/created.

Rise Of Seron

  • The Artificial Aetherian War erupts across the Zavila Galaxy.
  • The Vexx Empire creates Black Aether, a corrupted Aether variant.
  • The Guardians and Hell wage war against Norman Stillman.
  • New characters, factions, and upgrades appear across the galaxy.
  • The Black Prism vs Guardian War begins, fracturing alliances.
  • Petros Quinn Rogue — the Astral Monarch — is introduced.
  • The Varkuun Incident occurs: Ral’s Crimson Fear origin, Vexx invasion, and Anuket’s involvement.
  • The Universal War begins, pulling every faction into conflict.
  • More factions join The Guardians, forming the largest alliance in history.
  • The Final War of the Zavila Galaxy begins — every faction clashes.
  • Realizing the war is meaningless, all factions unite into the Unified Guardians.
  • Seron ascends, marking the end of the war era.

Ascension Of Seron

  • 10 years pass; the galaxy enters a peaceful era.
  • Most Guardians are worn out from decades of war.
  • Some retire; others become commanders, trainers, or advisors.
  • Seron partially retires to focus on family and peace.
  • The Zavila Galaxy stabilizes under the Unified Guardians.
  • Retired Guardians become civilians, explorers, or independent defenders.
